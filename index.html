Crypto Desk: A Live Research Desk for Digital Assets

Personal Project · Research, Analysis & Web Design

A public research desk that reads the crypto market the way an economist reads any asset class: fundamentals first, then the macro chain from oil and inflation to interest rates and risk appetite, then price structure. It covers Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Solana and BNB, combining live market data with written analysis.

  • Built live price, market-cap and candlestick charts on 1-hour, 4-hour and daily timeframes from free public data sources, with automatic fallbacks.
  • Computed technical indicators (moving averages, RSI, MACD, ADX, ATR) and risk metrics (beta, correlation, volatility) directly from raw price data.
  • Wrote the macro outlook, ETF and derivatives flows, an event calendar, per-coin investment theses and three price scenarios.
